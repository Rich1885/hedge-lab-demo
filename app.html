🌐 Multi-venue scanner

  • Asset — the symbol; ⚠ marks pairs where no combination has ≥$10k of 24h volume on both legs (thin book, size down).
  • Vari / Ethereal / Nado / Lighter / Lighter-RH / Aster / edgeX — that venue's current annualised funding rate (APR), each normalised from its own publishing convention and interval.
  • Coloured cells — the row's best pair: red border = short this leg (you receive funding), green = long this leg (you pay). Their difference is the spread.
  • Max size — the leverage cap of the narrower leg, with the margin that leg needs below it. A venue capping at ≤5× is telling you its book is thin — and on a thin book the price gap moves further than the funding pays.
  • Type — RWA / ALT / MAJOR. Funding behaves differently in each: RWAs settle on 8h intervals on Variational, alts mostly hourly elsewhere.
  • Everything else — spread, price gap, round-trip cost, break-even, 7-day history — is one click away in the deep dive, and ranked for you on Best Pairs.
